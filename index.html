DIFFERENT BINS

Our 3-tier custom segregation system designed inside the heavy-duty matte red storage bin shell.

Bin A

Broken & Unusable

Shredded circuit boards, cracked screens, and dead internal components headed straight for certified smelting.

Bin B

Broken but Usable

Functional keyboards, mice, and modular hardware candidate for student refurbishment and community donation.

Secure Core

Small Parts & Batteries

Lithium-ion phone batteries, loose cables, and small accessories stored safely in a sealed container.

THE E-WASTE REALITY

Why Project TUP-e-an exists. Electronic waste is the fastest-growing domestic waste stream on the planet.

⛏️

Urban Mining

There is 40 to 800 times more gold and precious metals in one metric ton of electronic circuit boards than in one metric ton of mined ore.

☠️

Toxic Leaching

When thrown in regular landfills, the lead, mercury, and cadmium inside gadgets seep into the soil, poisoning local groundwater and agriculture.

♻️

Circular Economy

By dropping devices in our bins, you prevent raw material extraction. We loop your tech back into the manufacturing cycle, saving energy and habitats.
